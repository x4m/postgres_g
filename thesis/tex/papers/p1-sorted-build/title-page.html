Acknowledgements

The work on non-leaf key overlap started as a distributed hackathon convened by Darafei Praliaskouski, who brought the problem — a spatial index that was built quickly and queried slowly — and gathered the participants. The problem itself was found and reported by Paul Ramsey, who measured the regression on PostGIS and published it; without that report the defect would have gone unnoticed for longer. Aliaksandr Kalenik and Sergei Shoulbakov took part in it; many alternatives were tried before the one described in the paper was chosen, and the discarded ones informed the design as much as the accepted one. I thank Heikki Linnakangas, Thomas Munro, Pavel Borisov, Andres Freund, Alexander Korotkov and Bernd Helmle for review of the implementation, Bernd Helmle for the btree_gist work discussed in the section on deployment experience, and Sergey Porshnev for discussions of the cost model.
