\noindent\textbf{Acknowledgements}

\medskip

\noindent
The work on non-leaf key overlap started as a distributed hackathon convened by
Darafei Praliaskouski, who brought the problem---a spatial index that was built
quickly and queried slowly---and gathered the participants. Aliaksandr Kalenik
and Sergei Shoulbakov took part in it; many alternatives were tried before the
one described in the paper was chosen, and the discarded ones informed the
design as much as the accepted one. I thank Heikki Linnakangas, Thomas Munro,
Pavel Borisov, Andres Freund, Alexander Korotkov and Bernd Helmle for review of
the implementation, Bernd Helmle for the \code{btree\_gist} work discussed in
the section on deployment experience, and Sergey Porshnev for discussions of the
cost model.
