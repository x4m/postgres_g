Document vacuum delay points called with interrupts held

vacuum_delay_point() cannot process pending interrupts when interrupts are
held.  Some call sites are reached while a buffer content lock is held, but
that constraint is not apparent at the call itself.

Mark the three such call sites observed by the regression and isolation test
suites with a common, grep-friendly comment.  Describe the lock responsible
at each site.  This changes no behavior.
